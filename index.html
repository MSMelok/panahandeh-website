01 — WHO WE ARE

A trading house built on
provenance and proof.

Panahandeh Foodstuff Trading L.L.C operates from Dubai — the world's most efficient crossroads for agricultural trade. We select raw material at origin, control it through packing, and move it into more than forty markets under a single standard.

Every consignment is inspected against destination-market requirements before it leaves. No substitutions, no surprises at the port.

Premium Quality

Carefully selected at origin and graded before packing — never blended down.

Global Partnership

Long-term relationships with growers, packers and distributors on five continents.

Safe & Natural

Pure, natural and healthy. 100% natural declaration on every retail format.
