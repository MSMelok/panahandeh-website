the ornament and use the letter alone, because the rings collapse into noise at that size.

Trading L.L.C — see the note at the top of the README.

## A note on the spice photography

Every "cumin" result available under the Pexels licence was either caraway seed or a styled
spoon shot, so the fourth spice line is **coriander seed** rather than cumin. If the client
trades cumin and wants it on the site, it needs a real photograph — substituting caraway
would be mislabelling a traded commodity.
