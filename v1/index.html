    03 — LOGISTICS & TERMS

    Consolidated at Jebel Ali.
    Shipped on your terms.

    Every consignment is consolidated at Jebel Ali, Dubai. We ship both FCL and LCL, and we build mixed-commodity containers — so a smaller buyer still moves a full load instead of waiting to reach volume on a single line.

    Lot codes and shipping documents are released together. The paperwork in your hand describes the pallet on the quay, not a template of it.

    INCOTERMS QUOTED

    FOB Jebel Ali

    Loaded on board at the port of loading; onward freight and insurance are yours to arrange.

    CFR

    Cost and freight through to the destination port you name.

    CIF

    Cost, freight and marine insurance through to the destination port you name.

    05 — ASSURANCE

    Certified at the lot.
    Documented for your port.

    Quality that only exists in the sales conversation is worth nothing at the port. Ours is written into the contract, measured before shipment and evidenced in the file that travels with the goods.

    CERTIFICATION & COMPLIANCE

    1. ISO 3632 category I for saffron, with lab results per lot. Colouring strength is stated, not estimated.

    2. Phytosanitary certificate and certificate of origin issued for every consignment.

    3. Halal certification where the destination market requires it.

    4. Food-grade packing materials throughout, retail and bulk alike.

    5. Documentation prepared against the destination market's requirement — not against a template.

    QUALITY ASSURANCE

    1. The crop is assessed in season, with the grower or the cleaner. Samples are pulled before any commitment is made.

    2. Sieved, de-stoned and colour-sorted to the calibration named in the contract.

    3. Moisture and foreign-matter limits are fixed in the contract, not negotiated on arrival.

    4. Pre-shipment inspection against destination-market requirements.

    TRACEABILITY

    One code, four answers. It is printed on the pouch and it is on the paperwork, so a buyer can resolve any pack back to the field it came out of.
