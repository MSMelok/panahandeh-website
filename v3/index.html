02 — Our Story

A house built on
provenance.

Saffron from Khorasan, chickpeas from Anatolia, lentils from the Kazakh steppe. We buy in season and at source, because that is the only point where quality is still a choice.

Panahandeh Foodstuff Trading L.L.C operates from Dubai, an hour's road from Jebel Ali — so a lot bought at origin can be graded, packed and stuffed without changing hands twice.
